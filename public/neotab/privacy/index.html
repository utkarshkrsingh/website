Privacy Policy for NeoTab

NeoTab does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data from users.

All data used by the extension, such as bookmarks and user preferences, is stored locally within the user’s browser using standard browser storage APIs.

NeoTab does not use any external servers, analytics tools, or tracking technologies.

No user data is shared with third parties.
